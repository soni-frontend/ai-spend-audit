# Project Unit Economics & Financial Viability

Understanding the operational cost and value generation models of the AI Spend Audit system.

## 1. Cost Structure (Operational Overheads)
- **Development & Maintenance Cost:** Minimal overhead achieved by utilizing lightweight client-side frameworks (Vite + React) that don't require heavy database hosting.
- **Hosting & Infrastructure Overhead:** $0/month initially by leveraging edge deployment topologies like Vercel for hosting static frontend assets.
- **Data Persistence Storage Overhead:** $0/month as the client telemetry details stay locally partitioned within the user's browser runtime (`localStorage`).

## 2. Customer Value Proposition (ROI)
- **Enterprise Subscription Bleed Reduction:** A standard medium-sized business spends roughly $1,500/month per team on fragmented AI software subscriptions. 
- **The Optimization Value:** By running an automated visibility audit, the system helps clear out overlapping tool footprints, generating a direct return on investment (ROI) within the first 10 minutes of operational assessment.

## 3. Future Monetization Vectors (Scalability)
- **Premium Team Tunnels:** Introducing encrypted cloud-coordinated dashboards for larger organizational teams to audit overlaps collaboratively.
- **Affiliate Integration Pipelines:** Recommending alternative open-source tools or bulk corporate plan pricing models via strategic platform partnerships.
